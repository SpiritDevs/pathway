# Panels and windows

Pathway can show several pages at once. Put pages side by side as **panels** in the main window, or open a page in its own **window**. Every panel and window is independent: two Email panels can show two different messages, and two chat panels can follow two different agents.

## Opening a panel

Drag an icon from the navigation rail into the page area. While you drag, the left and right thirds of the page area light up. Drop on one to open that page as a new panel on that side; the pages already open slide over to make room. Drop in the middle to open the page where you are, as a click does.

You can also right-click a rail icon and choose **Open in left panel** or **Open in right panel**, or press `Mod+Alt+\` to open the current page again beside itself.

How many panels fit depends on the width of your window. Each panel needs about 360 pixels, so a laptop fits two or three and an ultrawide monitor fits more. Panels that do not fit fold into a narrow tab at the edge of the window; click the tab to bring that panel back into view.

## Working with panels

One panel has focus at a time. It has a thin outline and a small bar at its top with the page name and its controls:

- **Flip** swaps the panel with its neighbour.
- **Open in window** moves the panel into its own window.
- **Close** closes the panel.

Click anywhere in a panel to focus it. The navigation rail, the sidebar, and the back and forward buttons all act on the focused panel, and so do keyboard shortcuts. Move focus with `Mod+Alt+Left` and `Mod+Alt+Right`, and close the focused panel with `Mod+Alt+W`.

Drag the line between two panels to resize them. Double-click it to give every panel an equal share. Double-clicking the edge of a thread's side panel works the same way: it takes half the width.

Panels are remembered on each device, so they are still there after you restart Pathway. A link you share opens only the page it points to, not your panels.

## Opening a window

Right-click a rail icon and choose **Open in new window**, or use the **Open in window** button on a panel. In the desktop app you can also drag a rail icon, or a panel's name, out past the edge of the window. The new window shows just that page, without the navigation rail.

Open as many windows as you like, including several of the same page. Windows reopen where you left them when you restart the desktop app. On macOS, windows stay open when you close the main window; click Pathway in the Dock to bring the main window back.

In a web browser, windows open as popups. If nothing appears, allow popups for Pathway in your browser.

## Closing panels and windows

Right-click a rail icon to close things by page:

- **Close panel** and **Close window** close the panels or windows showing that page. When more than one is open, choose which.
- **Close all panels** returns the main window to a single page.
- **Close all windows** closes every Pathway window except the main one.

A small dot under a rail icon means that page is open in a panel; a small ring means it has its own window.

The same actions are in the command palette, and in the desktop app's **View** and **Window** menus. All shortcuts can be changed in **Settings → Keybindings**.

Panels and windows are not available on phones.

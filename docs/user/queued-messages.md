# Editing queued messages on iOS

Editing a queued message saves its text and attachments on your device before cancelling the queued copy. Large attachments stay on disk while they are restored.

If the connection drops during cancellation, your edit remains saved, including after reopening the app. Sending stays disabled until Pathway confirms the original was cancelled. If cancellation cannot be confirmed, choose **Keep as new draft** and confirm that the original message may still run before sending a separate message.

When an attachment upload fails, **Retry** attempts the upload again, **Cancel** closes the alert and keeps the file, and **Remove** deletes it from the draft.

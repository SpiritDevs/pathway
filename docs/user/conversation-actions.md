# Organize conversations

Right-click a conversation, or focus it and press Shift+F10, to open its menu. Pin keeps it at the top. Mark as unread leaves a reminder until you select the conversation again. Hide alerts silences its notifications and removes it from notification counts; Show alerts turns them back on. These preferences are personal to you.

The conversation owner can archive or delete it. Both actions request that its unfinished agent work stop. Offline workers may take longer to confirm. You can read the conversation and see its stopping status under Archived conversations while confirmation is pending.

After an archive finishes, Restore conversation makes it available again. Cancelled work stays cancelled. Delete asks for confirmation and removes the conversation from your list once its work has stopped.

## Long tool output

Large command and tool results, tool inputs, file-change diffs, and subagent results show a preview with the start and end of the text. Choose **Show all** in the conversation details, or under a subagent's expanded result, to load the complete text from the connected environment. If the environment is disconnected, reconnect and try again. The complete text stays saved on the environment.

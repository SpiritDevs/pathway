# Your account

Pathway requires an account. The first time you open the app you'll be asked to sign in or
create one — everything else, on every device, lives behind that.

## Creating an account

Choose **Create an account** on the sign-in screen, enter your email and a password, and confirm
the six-digit code we send to that address. Codes expire after a short while; you can request a
new one from the same screen.

## Setting up your profile

After your email is confirmed, a short setup walks you through:

1. **You** — your name, and a profile photo if you'd like one. Only your first name is required;
   everything else can be skipped.
2. **How you'll use Pathway** — as an individual or as part of a company.
3. **A few details** — for companies: the company name, size, and your role. For individuals:
   which coding agents you use today and how you heard about us. All of it is skippable.

Setup remembers where you were. If you close the app partway through — even if you continue on a
different computer — it resumes at the step you left.

## Signing in on another device

Use the same email and password anywhere. Your name and photo follow your account
automatically.

## Forgot your password?

Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. We'll email a six-digit code; enter it along
with your new password and you're back in.

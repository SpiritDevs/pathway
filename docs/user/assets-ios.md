# Assets on iPhone and iPad

Assets are uploaded files that stay available across your devices. They belong to your company, and access follows the threads or tasks where they are attached. Files uploaded directly in Settings start private to you and company administrators.

## In conversations

A paperclip beside a thread means it has assets. Open the thread's **Assets** menu to view its files, upload an original, or attach an existing file. When reusing a file, the confirmation explains that readers of the destination thread gain access.

Images load into a reserved space and reveal briefly on first viewing. Reduce Motion skips the animation. Videos and audio play only when you tap Play. Playback pauses when the player scrolls out of view or the app becomes inactive. Open the gallery to move between images and videos.

**Uploading** and **Preparing preview** are separate states. You can download a verified original while its compatible preview is being prepared. A failed preview does not delete the original. If uploading fails, your local queued message and original remain available for retry.

## In Settings

Open **Settings → Assets** to search uploaded files, filter by type, uploader or date, change sorting, and check company storage usage. Use **Add asset** to upload a file. Use Select to manage several files together.

Open **Existing uploads** to review older task and conversation attachments. Authorized owners can create a private copy in Assets. The copy does not revoke old public links.

Open a file for its details, original download, attached locations, renaming and sharing. Renaming changes only the displayed name. A revised file is a separate asset; it never replaces what an older message originally showed.

**Remove from this thread** detaches one usage. **Delete asset** moves the asset to Trash for 30 days and immediately revokes share links. Restoring a file does not reactivate old share links. **Keep in Assets** retains a file independently of its attachments.

Authorized owners can create an external share link. Anyone with that link can access it until its selected expiry or until the owner revokes it. Links expire after seven days by default.

General files remain downloads. Supported documents can be previewed without running their contents. Asset upload limits and the limits of the selected AI provider are separate; a stored original may require a smaller compatible representation before an agent can read it.

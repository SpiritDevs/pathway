# Contacts

Contacts are shared with members of the selected workspace. Members with permission to manage the directory can add, edit, favorite, delete, and import contacts.

The directory loads 50 contacts at a time in name order. Choose **Load more contacts** to continue browsing, or **Favorites only** to narrow the directory. On Apple clients, Favorites is also available from the sidebar.

Search covers the entire workspace, including contacts you have not loaded. Choose **Name**, **Role**, **Company**, **Email**, or **Phone**, then enter words or a word prefix. Search matches appear in relevance order. Choose **Load more contacts** to see further matches.

An open contact stays up to date independently of the directory pages. To refresh previously loaded directory pages after changes on another device, pull down on Apple clients or choose **Refresh directory** on desktop.

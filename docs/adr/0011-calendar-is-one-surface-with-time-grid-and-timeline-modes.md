# Calendar is one surface with time-grid and timeline modes

`/calendar` carries four modes — Day, Week, Month, and Timeline — because a calendar and a Gantt answer different questions on the same dates: a time grid is hour-rows keyed on _when_, a timeline is item-rows keyed on _duration_, and neither survives being folded into the other or split into two panes. Timeline reuses the pure geometry already in `milestonesTimeline.logic.ts` and extends it: rows are projects expanding to their milestones, cycles render as a full-width band across the header, and issue due dates render as ticks on the project row. `/issues/milestones?view=timeline` stays exactly as it is and shares that logic, because it is project-scoped and belongs in issue context. Calendar events never appear on Timeline, where an hour-long event is a sub-pixel hairline; conversely issues, milestones, and cycles are date-only (`IssueDate`) so on the time grid they live in the all-day row alone, and dragging one writes a date and can never write a time.

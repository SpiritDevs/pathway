# Alert policy syncs but delivery settings are local

The global, project, thread, and per-event alert choices sync across a user's devices. Each device or
browser installation separately stores whether sound and operating-system notifications are enabled,
which built-in or system sound it uses, and any uploaded sound file. OS permissions and available
sounds belong to a client, and disabling sound on one computer must not disable iPhone push
notifications or change another computer. Custom audio does not require cloud storage under this
model.
